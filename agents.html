Who it's for

Students, POS agents, sales people, church and mosque members, market and association leaders, and anyone who knows business owners. You don't need to know anything technical: we do the talking, building and support. You just connect us.

The rules

  • You earn 10% of what a client you brought pays for their website or app build (deposit and balance) in their first 12 months. Yearly renewals and monthly services don't count.
  • Levels: bring more paying clients and your % goes up for every client after that (shown on your dashboard, e.g. from your 5th paying client).
  • A client is yours when they come through your link, when you register them on your dashboard first (yours for 90 days), or when they tell us your agent code before they pay. If two people introduce the same client, the first one counts.
  • Invite other agents with your invite link: when someone you invited earns their first commission, you get a bonus.
  • Your own business doesn't count, and refunds take back the commission on that money.
  • Ask for a payout once your balance is ₦5,000 or more. We pay to your bank within 7 working days.
  • Only promise what's on our website: our prices, what's included and the timing. Don't say you work for SIRRADDO, and never collect money from clients: they pay us directly.
  • No spam, fake reviews or misleading posts. We can pause an account that breaks these rules.

Questions

Does it cost anything to join?

No. It's free, with no targets.

Do I get flyers and videos to share?

Yes. Your dashboard has a promo kit: two WhatsApp Status pictures, an A5 flyer to print and a QR card, all with your own link, QR code and agent code (the flyer shows your name and number), plus 7 short videos with ready captions that carry your link. Tap Share to post them, or Save to print.

What if a business owner I talked to calls SIRRADDO directly?

Register them on your dashboard ("Register a business you're talking to") with their phone number before they contact us. They count as yours for 90 days, however they reach us.

Can I earn more than 10%?

Yes. As you bring more paying clients you move up levels (Silver, Gold) with a higher % on every client after that. We also give bonuses, for example to the top agent of the month, and a bonus when an agent you invited earns their first commission.

What do I show business owners?

Your dashboard has demo websites for salons, restaurants, churches, schools and online stores to open on your phone, a sales guide with answers to common worries, flyers and videos.

When do I get paid?

Your share counts as soon as the client's payment reaches us. Ask for a payout from your dashboard and we pay within 7 working days.

How do I know a client came through me?

Your dashboard lists every business that contacted us through your link, how far they've gone and what you've earned. We also email you when a new one comes in or pays.

What if the client calls instead of using my link?

Ask them to mention your agent code when they contact us, before they pay.

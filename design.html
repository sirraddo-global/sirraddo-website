FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take?

Flyers, business cards and invitations: usually 1–2 working days after payment. Social media packs 2–3, logos 3–5 and brand kits 5–7 working days. Need it faster? Message us on WhatsApp before you order.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Every design includes 2 rounds of changes (for example a different colour, wording or photo). Completely new ideas after that are quoted first.

Do you print?

No, we design. You get print-ready PDF and PNG files that any printer can use, and the right sizes for WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook.

I don't have a logo. Can you still make a flyer?

Yes. We can use your business name in a clean style, or you can order a logo design (or the brand kit) first.

Who owns the design?

You do, once it's paid for. Use it anywhere, as often as you like. Designs are original and made for your brand; we don't copy other businesses' logos, characters or photos.

How do I pay?

In full before we start, online by card, bank transfer or USSD through Paystack. You get an official receipt. See our payments and refunds policy.

Need something else designed?

Banners, menus, price lists, packaging: tell us what you need and we'll quote it.

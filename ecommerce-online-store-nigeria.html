FAQ

Questions we often get

Do I need a Paystack account?

Yes, in your business name. We connect your store to it, so every payment goes straight to you. Without it, the store can still take orders on WhatsApp.

Can I try a store first?

Yes. Open our demo store at sirraddodemo.com.ng (opens in a new tab) and place a test order.

What decides the final price?

Mostly the size of your catalogue. Tell us roughly how many products you sell and we send you a fixed quote.

How do I pay?

Usually 70% to start and 30% before your website goes live. For projects of ₦150,000 or more you can pay 50% to start, 30% when you approve your preview and 20% before going live.

Yes. The website has an admissions enquiry form with the details you ask for. Each enquiry comes to you by email, and parents can also message the school office on WhatsApp in one tap.

Can we post notices and change fees ourselves?

Yes. You can update notices, opening hours and phone numbers yourself, and we make other changes for you through your private link.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who owns my Google listing, domain and accounts?

You do. We set them up in your name and with your email, and guide you so you stay in control. We never keep your passwords.

How do the yearly and monthly renewals work?

After the first payment, the renewal is added to your account. We email you a reminder with a pay link a week before it's due. If you stop paying, the service pauses at the end of the period.

How fast is it?

Each service shows its usual time. Google's own checks for Maps listings can sometimes take longer; we follow it up for you.

How do I pay?

In full before we start, online by card, bank transfer or USSD through Paystack. You get an official receipt. See our payments and refunds policy.

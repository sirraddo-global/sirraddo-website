Payments and refunds

These rules apply when you pay us to build a website. To start a job you pay a 70% deposit, and you pay the balance before we hand over the website and its logins. Your invoice and receipts show what you have paid.

  • Before work starts (before you send us your project form): we refund the full deposit.
  • After work starts, before you see the preview: we refund 50% of the deposit.
  • After you approve the preview, or once the website is live: we do not refund. If something is wrong, we fix it at no extra cost.
  • Design orders (flyers, logos, social media designs, business cards and other designs, paid 100% upfront): full refund before we send your first design options; 50% refund after the first options are sent; no refund once you choose an option or we deliver the final files.
  • Domain names and yearly renewals: once we have paid the domain registrar or hosting provider for you, that fee cannot be refunded.

To ask for a refund, contact us on WhatsApp (+234 816 755 0937) or by email (info@sirraddo.com.ng) with your reference number (for example SGL-0001). We pay approved refunds to the account you paid from within 7 working days.

Instalments, discounts and quiet projects

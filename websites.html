FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take?

Usually 7 working days after we receive your completed project form, logo and photos.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo, photos, a short description of your business and your contact details. After your deposit we send you a simple online form that asks for everything, step by step.

How do I pay?

70% to start and the balance before your website goes live. Pay online from your invoice link (card, bank transfer, USSD or bank app, through Paystack), or by bank transfer to SIRRADDO GLOBAL LIMITED. You get an official receipt for every payment.

Will I see my website before it goes live?

Yes. We send you a private preview link. Look through every page, then tap Approve, or tell us what to change and we send an updated preview.

Can I edit the website myself?

You don't need to. We manage your website for you: send us the change on WhatsApp and we update it. Small fixes to text and photos are free for 30 days after launch; after that, we quote any change before we start.

Who owns my domain and website?

Your domain name is registered for your business, and your text, logo and photos remain yours. If you ever leave us, see "Can I move my website to another provider?" below.

What does the yearly renewal cover?

From year 2: your domain name, hosting, SSL certificate (the padlock), business email, backups and our daily check that your site is online. Starter is ₦25,000/year and Business is ₦40,000/year; Online Store renewal is quoted per store.

When is renewal due, and how will I know?

One year after your website goes live. We remind you 30 days before, a week before and on the day, by email and WhatsApp, with a link to pay online.

What happens if I don't renew?

Nothing is taken from you and you don't owe us anything for time you didn't use. Your website stays online for 14 days after the due date. After that, the website and business email are paused. We keep a copy of your website for 60 days, so you can switch it back on at any time by paying the renewal. Your domain name may expire if it isn't renewed: the domain company holds it for a short time, may charge a fee to restore it, and can later release it for anyone to register.

Can I move my website to another provider?

Yes. Once all payments are settled, we give you the code to transfer your domain and a copy of your website files, free of charge.

I already have a domain. Can you use it?

Yes. You can keep it where it is and we connect it to your new website, or move it to us so it renews with everything else.

Can I upgrade my package later?

Yes. Tell us what you need and we quote the upgrade, building on what you already have.

For online stores, where does the money go?

Straight to your own Paystack account. We set it up for you, and payouts go to your bank account. We never hold your customers' money. Your buyers add products to a basket, then pay online (card, transfer, USSD) or send the order to your WhatsApp. Paid orders are emailed to you and to the buyer.

Do you work with businesses outside Ibadan?

Yes, anywhere in Nigeria. Everything is done online and on WhatsApp.

What if I change my mind after paying?

See our payments and refunds policy: a full refund of the deposit before work starts, and 50% after work starts but before you see the preview.

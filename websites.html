    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    How long does it take?

    Usually 7 working days after we receive your completed project form, logo and photos.

    What do I need to provide?

    Your logo, photos, a short description of your business and your contact details. After your deposit we send you a simple online form that asks for everything, step by step.

    How do I pay?

    70% to start and the balance before your website goes live. Pay online from your invoice link (card, bank transfer, USSD or bank app, through Paystack), or by bank transfer to SIRRADDO GLOBAL LIMITED. You get an official receipt for every payment.

    Will I see my website before it goes live?

    Yes. We send you a private preview link. Look through every page, then tap Approve, or tell us what to change and we send an updated preview.

    Can I edit the website myself?

    For everyday things, yes. You get a private Manage link where you change, yourself and any time: product prices, how many you have in stock and sold-out items (online stores; paid online orders take the stock down by themselves and show "Only 2 left" to buyers), opening hours, phone and WhatsApp numbers, your slogan, a short notice on your website, your page background, and which customer reviews show. Filling stations and gas plants also update today's pump prices and stock (Available, Low stock, Out of stock). Tap Save and your website updates within a minute.

    For bigger changes (new products, pages, photos or text), send them with your private "Request a change" link or on WhatsApp and we do it for you. We send you a preview of your website with the changes first; when you're happy, tap "Put these changes live". Small fixes are free for 30 days after launch; after that, we quote any change before we start.

    Can my customers leave reviews on my website?

    Yes. Visitors give you 1 to 5 stars and a short review. Nothing shows until you approve it with your Manage link (we email you when one arrives), so fake or rude reviews never appear. Approved reviews show with your average rating.

    Will my website change when you improve your websites?

    From time to time we improve all the websites we build (new features, better looks), at no extra cost. Your text, photos, prices and colours stay the same, and we email you what changed. Prefer to approve updates first? Choose "Ask me first" in your Manage link. Don't like a change? Tell us and we'll adjust it for free.

    Can emails to my business addresses go to different people?

    Yes. Emails to addresses like info@yourbusiness.com.ng are forwarded to your Gmail, and some can go to someone else, for example receipts@ to your accountant. Each inbox confirms once with a link from our email provider. Replies go out from your own Gmail.

    Will you help with Google Maps and Search?

    Yes. On the Business website and Online Store packages, we guide you step by step to set up your free Google Business Profile, with a description written for you, and give you a short guide to keep it strong (photos, products, replying to reviews). It's yours: you own it and control it.

    Who owns my domain and website?

    Your domain name is registered for your business, and your text, logo and photos remain yours. If you ever leave us, see "Can I move my website to another provider?" below.

    What does the yearly renewal cover?

    From year 2: your domain name, hosting, SSL certificate (the padlock), business email, backups and our daily check that your site is online. Starter is ₦25,000/year and Business is ₦40,000/year (add ₦18,000/year if your domain is a .com); Online Store renewal is quoted per store.

    When is renewal due, and how will I know?

    One year after your website goes live. We remind you 30 days before, a week before and on the day, by email and WhatsApp, with a link to pay online.

    What happens if I don't renew?

    Nothing is taken from you and you don't owe us anything for time you didn't use. Your website stays online for 14 days after the due date. After that, the website and business email are paused. We keep a copy of your website for 60 days, so you can switch it back on at any time by paying the renewal. Your domain name may expire if it isn't renewed: the domain company holds it for a short time, may charge a fee to restore it, and can later release it for anyone to register.

    Can I move my website to another provider?

    Yes. Once all payments are settled, we give you the code to transfer your domain and a copy of your website files, free of charge.

    Can I have a .com instead of .com.ng?

    Yes. Every package includes a .com.ng domain, the Nigerian address, which tells customers you're a Nigerian business. A .com costs more to register and to renew, so it adds ₦18,000 in the first year and ₦18,000/year to your renewal. For example, Starter with a .com is ₦68,000 in the first year, then ₦43,000/year. The exact prices for every package are under Yearly renewal.

    Can my website be dark or colourful instead of plain white?

    Yes. Pick one of four page backgrounds: Brand tinted (a soft tint of your colours, the default), Midnight dark, Warm cream & gold, or Crisp light (white with bold coloured bands). Choose it in your project form, and change it any time in your Manage link. You can try all four on our demo websites with the buttons at the top.

    Do you build for my type of business?

    Yes. Each type gets a layout made for how it sells: restaurants and bakeries get a menu with an Order button on every dish; salons and fashion a lookbook; real estate a listings search with price filters; schools a "Book a school visit" form; clinics an appointment form; hotels and short-lets a check-in/check-out booking request; filling stations and gas plants a price board they update themselves and a bulk delivery request; churches online giving and testimonies; and online stores a full shop with payments. See them on our demo websites.

    What is the Monthly Care & Growth Plan?

    An optional plan after launch, ₦20,000/month, cancel any time: 4 promo flyers or WhatsApp status designs every month, monthly updates to your prices, photos and products, posts on your Google Business Profile, and priority WhatsApp support.

    Can I get a logo, flyers and business cards with my website?

    Yes. Add the Full Brand Starter Pack (custom logo, 5 social media flyers and a print-ready business card) to any website for ₦20,000. An animated WhatsApp status video is ₦8,000, and a business card design on its own is ₦5,000. Use the quote calculator to see your total.

    Can I upgrade my package later?

    Yes. Tell us what you need and we quote the upgrade, building on what you already have.

    For online stores, where does the money go?

    Straight to your own Paystack account. We set it up for you, and payouts go to your bank account. We never hold your customers' money. Your buyers add products to a cart, then pay online (card, transfer, USSD) or send the order to your WhatsApp. Paid orders are emailed to you. Buyers see their receipt right after paying, and can save it as a picture or PDF, share it, or open it again from its link.

    Do you work with businesses outside Ibadan?

    Yes, anywhere in Nigeria. Everything is done online and on WhatsApp.

    What if I change my mind after paying?

    See our payments and refunds policy: a full refund of the deposit before work starts, and 50% after work starts but before you see the preview.

    Can I pay in instalments?

    Yes. Usually you pay 70% to start and 30% before your website goes live. For projects of ₦150,000 or more you can pay 50% to start, 30% when you approve your preview and 20% before going live. Paying 100% upfront? You get 5% off.

    I already have a domain. Can you use it?

    Yes. You change two settings where you bought it (we show you how), or give us the transfer code. If you ever move to another developer, you get a copy of your website and your domain transfer code, free, once everything is paid.

    Will my website be on Google's first page?

    Nobody can honestly guarantee first place on Google. We submit your website to Google for you and build it to load fast on phones. What helps most is your free Google Business Profile with photos and customer reviews, and we guide you through setting it up.

    Do I need a mobile app?

    Most businesses don't. Your website works like an app: customers can add it to their phone's home screen in one tap. If you need customer logins, notifications or offline use, we can quote a custom app.

    Do you reward referrals?

    Yes. Once your website is live, you get your own referral link (it's on your Manage my website page). For every business that comes through your link and pays for a website, you get ₦5,000 off your next renewal.
